5 Everyday Headaches Fixed

The exact problems I solve for service owners

Every tool I build is designed around real bottlenecks in plumbing, cleaning, roofing, renovation, and rental teams.

01 Missed Calls

Missing customer calls while you're on a job

The problem: When you're driving or on-site and miss a call, that customer calls the next business on Google.

The fix: A fast callback & WhatsApp quote form on your website that captures the customer's name, number, and issue immediately.

02 Staff Dispatch

Texting job addresses & gate codes over WhatsApp

The problem: Spending every morning messaging staff where to go, leading to wrong addresses and constant phone calls.

The fix: One simple phone view where your crew sees today's job list, Google Maps directions, and customer notes in one tap.

03 Late Billing

Writing invoices at 9 PM & forgetting small parts

The problem: Paper job slips get lost, invoices go out days late, and extra materials often never get billed.

The fix: Technicians enter labor + parts on their phone and send a clean, itemized bill to the customer before leaving the driveway.

04 Online Trust

No website—or a site that looks broken on phones

The problem: High-budget customers check your website before calling. If you don't have one, they assume you're too small or unreliable.

The fix: A crisp, mobile-first website highlighting your services, reviews, and click-to-call button so local searches turn into real calls.

The problem: Not knowing which crew finished, who paid, and which invoices are still pending.
